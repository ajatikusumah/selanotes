About

I'm Vitra Janne Siregar — most people call me Janne. For more than two decades I've worked across Indonesia and Southeast Asia at the intersection of sustainable supply chains, forest conservation, trade and market access, rural livelihoods, and social inclusion.

I currently lead the regional technical implementation of the Tropical Forest Alliance strategy for the World Economic Forum, translating international sustainability requirements into practice that smallholders and subnational governments can actually work with. Alongside that, I design and run small enterprises in education, animal welfare, and community finance — built on the same conviction: that social purpose should be able to pay for itself.

Selanotes is named for my grandmother, Sela. It's where the policy work, the enterprises, and the writing that comes out of both live in one place.
